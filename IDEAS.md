# dear lover, dear heart, glass heart

## key themes:

- psychological horror
- doomed yuri
- exploration
- surreal
- time travel?
- reminiscence
- trauma
- glass

## main premise:

you arrive at your small apartment, after working a long shift at the office. you live in the city now, to get away from unpleasant memories, so you cant afford to live anywhere more lavish. you perform the same mundane tasks you always do: cook a small meal, put some clothes in the washing machine, take a shower, maybe vacuum the floor. then you go to sleep. on the first night, while asleep, you see a short flash of your beloved cottage, what you once had. the next day begins and you repeat the same tasks, except this time, you no longer have the will to shower. youre too tired. you go to sleep early. this time you dont wake up in the morning.

you are forever lost in a lucid dream, stuck in this memory of your home. but youre not aware youre dreaming. you tend to the cottage like normal, like you dont know what happened. as the days pass, your mind slowly remembers. you find clues about the narrative over time. you get worse, things start interacting in strange ways. you cant function like you used to.

what happened? you met a girl, you were so happy together, you loved eachother. you wanted to get away from the big city, so you ran away to a small cottage. you didnt have much here, but at least you had eachother. you had more than in the city. for a while, things were great. you loved eachother. you provided for eachother. but good things never last. her mental health wasnt great, and isolation from society exacerbated her issues. you were there for her, but it wasnt enough. on a tranquil day in spring, you get married. you were both elated beyond recognition. the next day, you find she has taken her own life. your whole life falls apart.

at the end of the game, you are presented two choices: knowing this information, will you also take your own life, or wake up and return to the big city like nothing happened? (no matter your choice, you die anyway)

the idea here is that throughout your time at the cottage, you find this information gradually, and its a sense of "audience equal position", where the main character knows just as much as you, the player, do. they uncover information whenever you uncover it. im not sure whether i want to add puzzle elements to the game, maybe you discover this information through actions rather than through puzzles giving you notes or something, because that sounds boring and doesnt fit with the lucid dream narrative as much as id like. the main theme of the game is glass, there may be objects shattering when you interact with them, your main cottage turning into glass randomly, surfaces becoming reflective and shattering when you look at them, etc. maybe when you learn an important piece of information, a glass heart will shatter as an overlay on your screen, as an indicator that this information is relevant. your goal as the player is to uncover the truth.

## phases

synopsis: the game should progress through phases, not set checkpoints. that way it can feel like the cottage is really alive rather than scripted changes each day. the sound design for each phase is extremely important (in fact for the entire game).

### phase 1: routine is normal

- cottage is warm and stable
- small differences such as an extra mug on the table, or a chair missing, or some sort of extra footsteps that dont match yours

### phase 2: signs that somebody else was there but is not now

- cooking for two but you come back to the dining room to only see 1 plate
- bed has a side that never wrinkles
- something dropped in another room

### phase 3: reality contradictions

- finding the wedding ring before being aware there was even a marriage
- flowers decay instantly when picked

### phase 4: hints of the past

- low pass laugh can be heard occasionally
- wedding hints

### phase 5: the truth bleeds through

- a room you couldnt enter previously can now be entered
- something is clearly wrong in that room (but must be subtle, show dont tell)

### phase 6: the air is unbreathable

- you start to piece together what happened
- you begin to suffocate and a heartbeat can be heard

## glass anomalies

synopsis: matching the story phases, the glass anomaly severity should also increase in phases.

### relation to the story:

- glass is fragile happiness, memories you cant hold
- love that shattered under pressure
- the idea to the player by the end is that the world isnt breaking, its already broken, but theyre only noticing it now

### system rules (to prevent it seeming scripted):

- glass reacts more when youre close to it
- repeated actions increase instability
- staring at things makes them worse (much like what she probably did during her traumatic event)
- something i want to call _observer damage_: looking at an object makes it worse, but not looking may return it to being intact _or_ may shatter it.
- don't rush the player. things should progress slowly.

### shatter/fracture intensity is relative to the emotional pressure

- how long you stay in certain spaces
- how often you repeat certain actions
- two players may see the same events but with different timings and intensity

### setpieces (transitions between phases)

1. someone was here with you

- you brew a cup of tea
- you place one cup down
- you turn to grab something
- when you turn back, there are two cups
- the second cup is faintly cracked
- if you pick it up, it cracks more in your hand
- if you ignore it, later you hear a soft crack sound: the cup cracked on its own

2. you were close

- you look out of the window
- you can see two shadow figures tending the garden together, no sound, no movement
- if you turn away and look back, theyre gone and its just you
- a crack slowly spreads throughout the glass, and it shatters when you leave the room

3. the wedding

- you find a wedding ring
- when you pick it up, the world becomes bright and all objects become intact
- after a few seconds, the ring becomes glass in your hands and cracks suddenly
- the first introduction of the glass heart shattering
- when the glass heart shatters, more objects have become glass than were there before you picked up the ring
- makes the player feel and understand how short-lived her marriage was

4. "the day after"

- after finding the wedding ring and sleeping, the next day you find that a room which was previously locked is now unlocked
- inside is a bedroom, a room that was previously not accessible.
- the room should be in a messy state (things on the floor, bed not made), but intact in terms of glass anomalies (has not been touched by her memories)
- as you enter, you can feel your screen shaking and becoming more intense, you understand that this room is important, something happened here, you just dont know what
- after a few seconds of being in the room, a light fixture begins glitching: the fixture may stretch slightly, or show tension marks, or form cracks downward. maybe even the walls can form cracks downward. something to indicate to the player that she died in this room. a chair is knocked over on the floor under the light fixture. glass shards on the floor?

5. denial

- you dont understand what you just saw
- as you walk through the cottage out of the room, things no longer shatter. objects that were glass remain to be glass, objects that were intact remain intact. as you look behind you however, you begin to notice that everything behind you has turned into glass.
- reinforces inevitability, highlights instability

### phase 1: barely noticeable

- you do a normal task, such as washing the dishes
- you put a glass down on the counter
- you hear a tiny "crack" sound
- you look back, the glass is cracked
- you pick up the glass, and its now perfectly intact
- at this point the player should be confused whether something even changed

### phase 2: glass responds to the player

- picking up glass objects may make them crack
- looking through windows for too long may make them crack
- nothing will fully shatter yet
- at this point the player should feel like her actions are causing these objects to crack

### phase 3: delayed shattering

- the object cracks when you hold it, then, only when you leave the room, it shatters. when you come back, its gone.
- at this point the player should feel like things are falling apart when shes not looking, exactly what happened to her in her relationship.

### phase 4: reflection anomalies

- mirror-like objects show incorrect states, like an empty room, or a past state of the room, or a room with extra objects.
- maybe object is cracked in the mirror but intact in real life, or vice versa
- at this point the player should feel like nothing theyre seeing is real anymore

### phase 5: structural glass

- the world around you begins turning into glass
- flowers you pick shatter in your hands
- objects like tables and chairs become translucent

### phase 6: fracture events

- when the player learns important information, a glass heart overlay will appear on their screen, and slowly crack, then shatter. when the heart shatters, various objects around them in the current room theyre in will also shatter. this should be _only_ used for important information, and should maybe be used up to 3 times in the entire game.
- at this point the player should feel like something just shattered inside themselves.

### phase 7: losing grip on reality

- rooms should constantly glitch between being intact/some objects being glass/the entire room is glass
- walking between rooms may shatter the screen like glass
- shards of glass suspended in midair like particles
- at this point the player should feel like this cottage is gone, and they are left in a corpse of what once was, a shattered visage of the life they used to dream of.

### phase 8: stillness (death state)

- no more shattering
- all objects return to being intact
- one by one, all objects begin turning into glass
- at this point, the player should feel like they have reached the end, it just hasnt ended yet

## when you die

(cutscene) if you chose to take your own life, the cottage becomes calm and still, the glass stops shattering and dissapears. slowly, you watch as everything around you turns to glass. you walk over slowly, and lay down where she died. the screen cuts to black, and you hear a shatter sound.

if you chose to return to the city life, you return to the city apartment, but things behave in strange ways. glass objects shatter when you look at them, you act clumsy, dropping items randomly. maybe things turn to glass randomly. the sound of something shattering can be heard distantly. finally, you are done with your daily chores and you sleep. then, you go to sleep, and the same black screen with a shatter sound can be heard.

in both cases, after the fact, you can see the camera pan outwards into a shattered framed photod of the couple on the floor, and next to it, a newspaper. depending on your choice, the newspaper will say "local woman found deceased in apartment" or "local woman has gone missing".
